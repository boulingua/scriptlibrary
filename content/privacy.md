## Analytics

Plausible Analytics is disabled on this website.
No Plausible analytics script is loaded. Pageviews, file downloads and outbound
link clicks are not reported to a Plausible endpoint. Hosting and any other
services are described separately in this notice.


The reading room has no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or accounts. Books, fonts and scripts are served from the same site. Selecting a practice alphabet and density stores those settings in this browser's local storage; clearing site data removes them. The reader does not store your reading history or send your selections to a server.

Downloading or opening the website makes normal HTTP requests to its host. When deployed on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes those requests under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). External source links contact their respective sites only when you follow them.

The optional Chrome extension stores preferences locally and requests extra site access only when you enable automatic practice for that site. It does not transmit page contents.
