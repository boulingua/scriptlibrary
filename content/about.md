---
title: How this reading room works
---

Read a book you can already understand, with a small share of its letters drawn from another alphabet. The words stay in the original language. The aim is to become comfortable seeing unfamiliar letter forms during sustained reading.

Start with **20% of eligible letters**, then increase or decrease the slider. Choose **Original text** at any time. Choose Greek, one shared Cyrillic introduction, Nordic, Polish or Czech. The Nordic set uses selected Norwegian/Danish and Swedish matches; Icelandic differences are explained separately.

The sound matches are approximate and depend on the source language. For example, Modern Greek **β** represents a v-like sound, while Ukrainian **ґ** corresponds to hard g (explained in the introduction, excluded from the shared Cyrillic subset). German **ö** can be practised as Norwegian **ø**. German ö is **not** changed to Russian ё, which has a different sound.

Some letters and contexts are deliberately skipped. The percentage is a sample of eligible letters, so 20% does not mean one fifth of all characters will change. Every PDF gives its actual replacement counts. An unchanged copy of every book is available.

The online reader remembers only your chosen alphabet and density in this browser. Text and fonts are hosted with the library. No account, analytics or third-party font service is used. Screen readers and pronunciation tools should use the original text: mixed scripts can change how they speak.

The downloads contain complete original-language narratives, not excerpts. *Candide* omits the later editorial preface and footnotes; the Alice edition omits illustrations. Historical spelling is retained. These are reading exercises, not new translations, phonetic transcriptions, or claims of curriculum conformance.

The same versioned mapping engine powers the [Chrome extension](../extension/), the online reader and the generated PDFs. The supplied Azbuka prototype contributed the ideas of staged introduction and an immediate original-text view.


## Fixed and increasing editions

Every book offers original text plus 20%, 100% and progressive 20–100% PDFs for all five practice sets. In the progressive edition, the replacement rate rises linearly by paragraph position from the beginning to the end of the entire work. It does not restart at chapters or depend on PDF page count. The online “Increasing 20–100%” mode uses exactly the same schedule and stable samples.

## A simplified learning exercise

For learning purposes only: spelling, pronunciation, stress, dialect, rhythm and usage are only partly represented. These editions do not teach a complete alphabet or language and do not replace pronunciation instruction. 100% replaces all eligible letters, not every character. The shared Cyrillic set deliberately omits letters whose sound or use differs between national alphabets. The Nordic label groups a few useful matches, not a unified alphabet.

**Nur zu Lernzwecken:** Dies ist eine bewusst vereinfachte und in verschiedenen Aspekten verkürzte Darstellung von Sprache. Aussprache, Rechtschreibung, Betonung, regionale Varianten und sprachliche Zusammenhänge werden nur teilweise berücksichtigt. Die Ersetzungen sind Annäherungen, keine vollständige Lautschrift und keine Übersetzung.
