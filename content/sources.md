The original literary texts included here are public domain in Germany and the United States. Each source catalogue explicitly identifies its US public-domain status. The German assessment uses the authors' death dates and [UrhG §§64 and 69](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/BJNR012730965.html). These are original-language narratives, without modern translations or illustrations. Copyright terms can differ elsewhere.

Each PDF reproduces the complete **Project Gutenberg licence**, unchanged, and its source notice. The reader links to the full licence and a free original-text download for each book. Files are distributed without charge. See [the source licence](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license) and [redistribution guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission).

*Candide* contains all thirty narrative chapters but omits the later editorial preface, editorial notes and note numbers. Alice contains all twelve chapters, with verse and emphasis retained, without illustrations. Kafka contains all three narrative parts. Source publisher front matter is omitted from the reading text; original source files are preserved in the repository.

The original texts and purely mechanical substitutions are not relicensed as exclusive new works. Website code, generators and the extension are **MIT**. New explanatory editorial material is **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Embedded Noto Serif and Source Sans 3 fonts retain their **SIL Open Font License 1.1** notices. No VG Wort marks are assigned to public-domain narratives.

The repository records each retrieved source's SHA-256 digest, extraction policy, chapter count, engine version and generated PDF digest. This records exactly which source and rules produced an edition.
