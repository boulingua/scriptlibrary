The repository records each retrieved source's SHA-256 digest, extraction policy, chapter count, engine version and generated PDF digest. This records exactly which source and rules produced an edition.

## Complete works and extraction scope

**Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland:** Complete twelve-chapter original English text. A text-only edition: source illustrations are omitted. Paragraphs, verse line breaks and italic emphasis are retained.

**Candide, ou l’optimisme:** Complete thirty-chapter original French narrative. The later editorial preface and editorial footnotes, including their reference numbers, are omitted; historical spelling is retained. This is not a modern translation.

**Die Verwandlung:** Complete three-part original German narrative. The source publisher’s front matter is omitted. Historical spelling and paragraph divisions are retained.

**Faust I:** Complete Faust: Der Tragödie erster Teil, including Zueignung, Vorspiel auf dem Theater and Prolog im Himmel. All 25 dramatic scenes, verse lines, speakers and stage directions are retained; the standalone part-title is not a separate chapter.

**Der Schimmelreiter:** Complete continuous German narrative, including its framing story. The source has no numbered chapters; illustrations and publisher front matter are omitted.

**Hamlet:** Complete original English play: dramatis personae and all 20 scenes across five acts. Speaker names, stage directions and source line breaks are retained. Text from Project Gutenberg 1524, not the separately restricted World Library electronic edition.

**A Christmas Carol:** Complete five-stave original English narrative. Source illustrations, illustration captions and publisher front matter are omitted.

**Eugénie Grandet:** Complete continuous French novel, with Balzac’s dedication. The frozen source has no numbered chapters. Historical spelling and paragraphs are retained.

**Le Horla et autres nouvelles:** Complete 1887 French collection: Le Horla and all thirteen accompanying stories. Amour retains its subtitle Trois pages du livre d’un chasseur. Historical spelling, emphasis and paragraph divisions are retained.

**Der Sandmann:** Complete German story Der Sandmann extracted from Nachtstücke, from its title to immediately before Ignaz Denner. The three opening letters and the concluding narrative are retained. The other stories in the source collection are not included.

**Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde:** Complete ten-chapter original English text of Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde. Source chapter headings and paragraph divisions are retained.

**Trois contes:** Complete French collection: Un cœur simple (five sections), La Légende de saint Julien l’Hospitalier (three sections), and Hérodias (three sections). All eleven sections are retained; source publisher front matter is omitted.

## Requested works not included

Camus died in 1960 and his works have not completed Germany’s ordinary 70-year post-mortem term. [BnF author record](https://www.bnf.fr/fr/albert-camus-1913-1960-bibliographie), [UrhG §64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html). Le Petit Prince remains subject to country-specific restrictions, including France; it is not included in this freely downloadable collection. [Bibliothèque municipale de Lyon rights explanation](https://www.guichetdusavoir.org/question/voir/136439). Maupassant is the freely reusable alternative selected here.
