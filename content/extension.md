---
title: Practise on other websites
---

**Scriptbridge** brings the same letter practice to ordinary websites. Choose the page's source language, a target alphabet, a letter set, and the percentage of eligible letters to replace.

[Download the Chrome extension ZIP](../downloads/scriptbridge-0.1.0.zip).

1. Unpack the ZIP into a folder you will keep.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome or Chromium.
3. Enable **Developer mode** and choose **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the folder containing `manifest.json`.
5. Open a normal website, click Scriptbridge and choose **Apply to this page**.

Hold **Alt** on the page to peek at the original. **Show original** restores the text. Enable **Always practise on this site** if you want practice to resume on future visits; Chrome asks for that site's permission.

The extension is available for local installation. It is not listed in the Chrome Web Store. Processing is entirely local; no page text or reading history is sent anywhere. Forms and editable text remain untouched.
